Free Online Image Compressor

ToolNest India's Image Compressor helps you reduce image file size quickly and easily. You can select JPG, PNG or other image files and compress them directly in your browser.

How to Compress an Image?

  1. Select your image.
  2. Choose the image quality.
  3. Select the output format.
  4. Click "Compress Image".
  5. Download your compressed image.

Is my image uploaded?

No. The compression is performed directly in your browser using JavaScript and Canvas. Your image does not need to be uploaded to a server.
